Implementation (GitHub issues A01–A10):

I was primarily responsible for the ECS entity management, physics, player movement, and collision detection systems. My contributions included:

Additional Features:
- Continuous Collision Detection: Used Swept AABB to detect collisions along movement paths, reducing the possibility of fast-moving entities passing through obstacles.
- Collision State Tracking: Added previous-frame collision information and Begin/Stay contact events to support wall jumping and prevent repeated collision-triggered gameplay effects.
- Automated Testing: Expanded EngineTest.cpp with unit and integration tests covering entity management, gravity, movement, collision detection, wall jumping, and interactions between systems.

Design Decisions and Debugging:
- Component Management: Used compact component storage and EntityID reuse to manage game objects efficiently while checking entity validity during system updates.
- Separated Physics Systems: Kept gravity, movement, input, and collision handling in separate systems to improve modularity and make individual behaviors easier to test.
